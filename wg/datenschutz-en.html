Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Deutsch

This policy explains which data the app “Nearer Flat” processes, why, and what rights you have.

In short

  • No tracking, no ads, no sale of your data.
  • Without an account, all data (chores, shopping list, expenses, events, answers, photos, game scores) stays on your iPhone.
  • Only if you share your flat online does the app create an anonymous account, without name, email or password. We then store the flat’s content and photos on our server in Frankfurt, visible only to the members of your flat.
  • You can delete your online account in the app at any time.

2. Data that stays on your device

As long as you do not share the flat online, the app stores everything only on your iPhone: flatmate names, chores with points, shopping list, expenses, calendar events (including from an imported .ics file or from your iPhone’s Calendar app), answers to the daily question, scratch card, game scores and polaroid photos. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Calendar access: Only if you choose “Import from Apple Calendar” and allow access does the app read the events of the next 90 days from the calendars you select. These events stay on your iPhone and are not sent to our server or shared with your flat. You can revoke access at any time in the iPhone settings.

Reminders and the widget run locally on your device and send nothing to us. When you import a calendar by link, your iPhone contacts the address you entered yourself. No data about you is sent along.

3. Sharing online and sign-in

Sign in with Apple (optional). When sharing online you can sign in with Apple so you can restore your flat on a new phone. Apple does not pass us a name or email address; we only receive an app-specific identifier. We store it to assign your account to the flat. You can end the sign-in at any time in iOS Settings (Apple Account, Sign in with Apple); you delete your online account in the app. Deleting it also revokes the link at Apple. For this we store a token issued by Apple until deletion, which is then deleted as well.

Without signing in. Otherwise the app creates an anonymous account in the background.

If you create or join a flat online, the app creates an anonymous account in the background. Our server assigns a random user ID; there is no name, email address or password. The app keeps the sign-in key in your iPhone’s keychain. We then store on our server (Supabase, Frankfurt):

  • your user ID and your flat membership,
  • the flat’s invite code,
  • the content you share in the app: flatmate names with colour, move-in date and planned absences, the optional PayPal.Me name, chores with the done counter, shopping list, expenses and fixed costs, events with checks and feedback, pinboard notes (for example Wi-Fi details you enter there yourself), guest announcements with the guest’s name, answers to the daily question, scratch card state, weekly game results,
  • the household type (single, flat share or family) and the end date of a group subscription (see section 9),
  • the polaroid photos you upload.

All members of your flat can see this content. Nobody else has access; access rules on the server enforce this. If you leave the flat, you lose access. If all members leave, we delete the flat with its content. Do not enter anything on the pinboard that not everyone in your flat should see, and only enter other people’s names (for example guests) if they agree.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you use).

4. Service providers

  • Supabase. Database, anonymous account and file storage in Frankfurt. Supabase processes data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). The data is stored in Frankfurt. Because Supabase Inc. is based in the USA, any transfer there relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple. Distribution of the app (App Store, TestFlight). Apple’s privacy policy applies.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) serves this website. When you open it, GitHub technically processes your IP address. The app itself loads nothing from the website. The transfer to the USA relies on the EU adequacy decision (Data Privacy Framework) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the app under Settings → Share online → “Delete online account” you delete your account. You leave all flats and your account disappears from the server. Flats without members are deleted with their content. Data on your iPhone stays there until you delete the app. We also delete data on request by email.

6. Retention

We keep data until you delete it, the flat or your account. Backups at our provider are overwritten after seven days at the latest.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing (Art. 21) and withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)). Write to nearer.app@outlook.com.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority, for example the Data Protection Commissioner of Lower Saxony, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany (www.lfd.niedersachsen.de).

8. Minimum age, children and security

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. Children under 16 do not use it themselves and do not create an account. In the family household, parents or guardians may enter their children’s first names or nicknames as household members, for example for chores. They do so on their own responsibility, enter no more data than necessary and do not upload photos of children. If the flat is shared online, these names are stored like the other content (section 3), and the parents can change or delete them at any time. If we learn that a child under 16 has created their own online account, we delete it. Parents can reach us at nearer.app@outlook.com.

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). On the server only members of a flat can read that flat’s content.

9. Subscription (Nearer Flat Plus)

An optional subscription is available. Purchase and payment run through Apple (in-app purchase). We do not receive payment data, we only see whether and until when a subscription is active. For a group subscription (flat or family) the end date is stored with the flat together with the household type so everyone in the flat can use Plus. The legal basis is performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can cancel any time in your Apple ID settings under Subscriptions.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is always on this page.
