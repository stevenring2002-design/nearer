Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains which personal data we process when you use the app "Nearer", what we use it for and what rights you have.

At a glance

  • No tracking, no ads, no selling of your data.
  • Your names, your anniversary, your flower history and your gender information stay on your iPhone. If you connect with your partner, we store your answers to the question of the day and the daily quiz, your wishlists, hearts you send, peace offers, streak rescues, time-outs and surprise dates on our server – visible only to the two of you, and the other's answer only once you've answered yourself.
  • Ideas you share are visible to all users after our review – with your display name.
  • Our server is located in London (United Kingdom). The European Commission has issued an adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

2. Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following data only on your iPhone. We do not receive it:

  • Flower counter: logged flowers, memory photos, notes and the next flower day – never on our server, even with a partner connection.
  • Information about you and your sweetheart: your gender and your partner's gender, if you provide them. This information is optional and only shapes suggestions in the app, such as the gift filter.
  • "Us" section: your first names, the date you got together, your wishes from setup, your answers to the question of the day and the quiz, your streak, your wishlist, the year in review and your partner's birthday if you enter it for the reminder (exception: partner connection, see section 6).
  • Settings such as reminders and visible sections.

The app schedules reminders for flower day and for the question of the day as local notifications directly on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted as well.

3. Your account

For the partner connection and for sharing ideas, the app creates an account in the background – without a name, email address or password. Our server assigns it a random identifier; the app keeps the sign-in key on your iPhone in the iOS Keychain. As your display name we use the first name you enter in the app.

All other features – the question of the day for two on one iPhone, the flower counter, browsing and bookmarking ideas – work without an account.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app under our terms of use).

4. Shared ideas, photos and ratings

When you share a date or gift idea, we store the title, description (including its translation), category, budget, place or occasion and recipient, the time and your display name – but no photos. Once approved, this content is visible to all users of the app. Your ratings (hearts), saved ideas and markers such as "tried" or "given" stay on your iPhone (exception: shared dates with a partner connection, see section 6).

So that all users can read your idea, the app automatically translates the title and description into English or German when you share it. This happens with the iOS translation feature directly on your iPhone; the text is not sent to us or to third parties for this. We store and publish the translation together with your idea and review it during approval.

Your own photos that you add to an idea as a memory are stored only on your iPhone – they are not sent to us or to your partner. When you add them, they are downscaled and embedded metadata such as the location is removed.

The sample and mood images (photos from Unsplash) are included in the app. Displaying them sends no data to Unsplash or any other service.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. Review, reports and blocking

To keep objectionable, offensive or illegal content out, our team reviews new ideas before publication. If you report an idea, we store the report with its reason and time. If you report a message in the game chat, we store a copy of the message with its sender, reason and time so that we can review it. If we reject or remove content, we tell you why. If you block someone, we store this so that their content is no longer shown to you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a safe community) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with the Digital Services Act.

6. Partner connection

You can connect with your partner using an invite code. For this we store on our server: your random account identifiers, the connection, the first name you entered in the app (as the display name the other person sees), the invite code (for at most 24 hours) and your answers to the question of the day with date and question, and – for date ideas – which ones you've bookmarked and which you've done together (only the idea's identifier). Also: your answer and your guess in the daily quiz, the entries on your wishlist (title and details), which of the other person's wishes you've marked as "I'll get this", hearts you send (only the time), cuddle alerts (who asked, when, and whether the other person said yes), games (which game, game state and moves, the stake, the result, and for Sea Battle where your ships are, for Word Duel your word and for Draw & Guess your drawing with the term – the other person only sees ships, word and term after the game ends), messages in the game chat (text, sender, time and which game – only the two of you can read them), peace offers (only when you tapped and whether you both did – the other person sees your open offer and gets a notification), streak rescues (which day you saved, which task you confirmed and when), time-outs (who started them, start and end), surprise dates (title, time and your hints with their times – the other person only gets each hint at its time and the title when the date begins), planned dates (the idea's identifier and the time) and, for your streak, the days on which you both answered (only the date, without the answers). If you enter a wrong invite code, we store the time of the failed attempt to prevent guessing other people's codes. Gift ideas, ratings and photos stay on your iPhone.

Only the two of you can access this data. The server only releases the other person's answer – for the question and the quiz – once you have answered for the same day yourself. What you mark as "I'll get this" on the other person's wishlist is visible only to you – they never find out. Saved answers cannot be changed afterwards. Either side can disconnect at any time – we then delete the connection and all shared answers from the server; whatever is stored on your iPhones stays there.

Answers and quiz guesses stay on our server for at most three days, hearts and peace offers for at most two days, streak rescues for at most 30 days, time-outs for at most one day after they end, cuddle alerts for at most one day, games for at most 30 days after they end or after the last move, and surprise dates for at most two days after they begin, and are then deleted automatically – your question diary is stored on your iPhones. The days of your streak stay on the server while you're connected – so your streak is kept on a new iPhone, too. The wishlist stays on the server while you're connected; if you delete a wish or disconnect, it is deleted there. We never transfer flowers, flower photos or notes.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Notifications and crash reports

Reminders for the question of the day and flower day are local notifications that the app schedules directly on your iPhone. We store nothing on our server for these.

If you are connected with your partner and have allowed notifications, you receive push notifications when the other person answers the question of the day, sends you a heart, wants to cuddle or says yes to cuddling, challenges you to a game, makes a move or finishes a game, starts a time-out, makes or accepts a peace offer, plans a date, saves the same date as you, adds a new wish, or plans a surprise for you. For this we store your iPhone's push token (a device identifier issued by Apple) together with your account on our server. The notification itself is delivered by the Apple Push Notification service (APNs); only the type of event and the sender's display name are passed to Apple – no answers, wishes or details of the surprise. The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

If you have agreed in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with anonymized crash reports. We only use them to fix bugs.

8. Premium subscription

You take out the Premium subscription through the App Store; payment, invoicing, renewal and cancellation are handled exclusively by Apple. We do not receive your payment details, your name or your Apple ID.

The app checks whether your subscription is active directly on your device using purchase receipts signed by Apple. We process this information (subscription type, period, status) only to unlock Premium and currently do not transfer it to our server.

Apple also provides us with aggregated sales reports without personal data, such as the number and country of subscriptions.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the subscription contract).

9. Service providers and transfers to third countries

We use the following service providers:

  • Supabase Inc. – server, database and sign-in for the partner connection and shared ideas; storage location London (United Kingdom; EU adequacy decision). Supabase processes the data on our behalf.
  • GitHub Inc. (USA) – hosting of our website on GitHub Pages (support page, privacy policy, terms of use, legal notice). When you visit the website, GitHub technically processes your IP address as well as browser and time information in server logs in order to deliver the pages and protect them against abuse. We use no cookies and no tracking on the website; the app itself loads nothing from the website. The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably).
  • Web shop and partners such as Amazon (Amazon EU S.à r.l., Luxembourg) – only if you tap a date kit or a partner link. The provider's page then opens in the browser; the app does not transmit any data about you. The link only contains our partner ID so the provider can attribute a purchase to us and pay a commission. What happens there (order, payment, cookies) is the provider's responsibility under its own privacy policy.
  • Microsoft (Outlook.com) – our email inbox. If you write to us, we process your email address and message only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once your request has been dealt with. The support email from the app includes the app and iOS version and the device model to help with troubleshooting – you can delete this line before sending.

A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Supabase (Data Processing Addendum as part of the Supabase terms of service). Where Supabase or its sub-processors transfer data to the USA, this is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

Apple itself is responsible for the App Store, downloads, payments, delivery of push notifications (APNs) and on-device translation; Apple's privacy policy applies to these.

10. Retention

  • We store account data, shared ideas, ratings and the partner connection until you delete the content, the connection or your account.
  • We automatically delete answers to the question of the day and quiz guesses from the server after three days at the latest, hearts and peace offers after two days, streak rescues after 30 days, time-outs one day after they end, cuddle alerts after one day, games including their chat messages 30 days after they end or after the last move, and surprise dates two days after they begin.
  • We store wishlists until you delete the wish, disconnect or delete your account.
  • We delete planned dates at the latest two days after the planned time or when you cancel them.
  • We store the days of your streak until you disconnect or delete your account.
  • We delete failed invite code attempts after one day.
  • We store your iPhone's push token until you delete your account or Apple reports it as invalid (for example after you delete the app). We delete pending push notifications after one day.
  • We store rejected ideas and reports (including reported chat messages) for up to 6 months to be able to trace abuse.
  • Our server provider's backups are overwritten after 7 days at the latest.
  • We store purchase information as long as it is needed for unlocking or as required by statutory retention periods.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account data, shared ideas, photos, ratings and partner data. You cancel an active subscription in your Apple ID settings.

12. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Just write to us at nearer.app@outlook.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for our place of business: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen (data protection authority of Lower Saxony), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany (www.lfd.niedersachsen.de).

13. Minimum age

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

14. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Data stored on the iPhone is protected by iOS file encryption. On the server, access rules ensure that only authorized people can see data – your partner's answer, for example, only once you've answered yourself.

15. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. You can always find the current version in the app under Profile → Settings → Privacy policy.
